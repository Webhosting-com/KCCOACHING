About KC Coaching

Football is just the starting point

At KC Coaching, we do more than teach football — we help young people grow through it. Based in Gloucestershire, we coach children of all ages and abilities, from toddlers taking their first kick to academy-level players chasing their next breakthrough.

Through after-school clubs, group sessions, one-to-one coaching and holiday camps, our mission is to deliver football experiences that are fun, developmental and built on trust. Our coaches bring real playing experience from clubs including Cheltenham Town, Forest Green Rovers, Oxford United and Bristol Rovers.

At the heart of everything is positive coaching, tailored development and creating opportunities on and off the pitch.

Good to know

FAQs

What age can my child start?

From age 2. Toddler sessions run for ages 2–5, group sessions for ages 6–12, and academy groups for ages 7–16. 1-to-1 coaching is available for all ages.

Do you run girls-only sessions?

Yes — girls-only groups for ages 6–12 run in Gloucester (Wingate, Monday & Thursday) and Cheltenham (Tuesday & Thursday), 6–7pm.

Who are academy group sessions for?

Players currently signed to or training with professional or top semi-professional clubs who want to stay sharp and keep progressing throughout the season.

How does booking and payment work?

Use our booking page to choose a session and send your details to the team. To secure a place on a weekly session and pay, you'll be taken to our ClassForKids booking system. 1-to-1 and camp requests are confirmed directly by the team.

Are your coaches qualified and checked?

All coaches are DBS checked and safeguarding assured, and sessions are fully insured with public liability cover and first aid compliant. Our coaching team holds FA and UEFA qualifications.

Do you offer goalkeeper coaching?

Yes — specialist goalkeeper coaching is available on a 1-to-1 basis with our dedicated GK coaches.

Privacy notice

KC Coaching Development Ltd

We collect the details you give us when you book or enquire: your name, phone number and email, and your child's name, date of birth, club, experience and any medical information or additional needs.

Why we use it

  • To manage bookings and contact you about sessions
  • To place your child in the right age group
  • To keep your child safe — medical details are shared only with the coaches running their session
  • Photos and videos are only used on social media if you've given consent

How it's kept

Booking details are sent securely to our team by email or WhatsApp, and payments are handled by ClassForKids. We don't sell your data or share it for marketing. We keep it only as long as your child trains with us, plus any period required for insurance or safeguarding records.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing kccoaching@outlook.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
