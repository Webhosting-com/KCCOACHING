About KC Coaching

Football is just the starting point

At KC Coaching, we do more than teach football — we help players develop on and off the pitch. Based in Gloucestershire, we coach players of all ages and abilities, from toddlers taking their first kick to aspiring academy players and adults looking to take their game further.

Our programmes include after-school clubs, group training, one-to-one coaching and holiday camps. Every session is designed to be enjoyable, purposeful and tailored to the player, helping them build skill, confidence and a lasting love of the game.

Our coaching team brings experience and connections across the game. With strong agency relationships and a commitment to positive coaching, individual development and opportunity, we support every player to reach their potential.

Parent info

Before your first session

Everything you need to know so your child turns up ready to play.

🎒

What to bring

  • Football boots or trainers (astro trainers for 3G)
  • Shin pads
  • A named water bottle
  • Weather-appropriate kit — layers in winter
  • Goalkeepers: gloves
  • Medical needs: bring any medication your child may need (e.g. inhaler or EpiPen) and tell the coach about any medical conditions or additional needs
📅

Booking & cancellations

  • Weekly sessions are booked and paid on ClassForKids — most start with a free trial
  • Can't make it? Let us know at least 24 hours before
  • If we cancel for weather or venue issues, you won't be charged
  • Arrive 5 minutes early for registration
🛡️

Safeguarding

  • All coaches enhanced DBS checked & safeguarding trained
  • First-aid trained staff at every session
  • Fully insured with public liability cover
  • Children are only released to a known adult

Good to know

FAQs

What age can my child start?

From age 2. Toddler sessions are for ages 2–5, group sessions 6–12, girls-only 7–13, elite groups U7–U16, goalkeeper groups 6–18 and S&C 7–18. 1-to-1 coaching is available from age 5 — and for adults.

Do you coach adults?

Yes. Adults looking for coaching for themselves can book 1-to-1 sessions — send us an enquiry or message us on WhatsApp.

How does booking and payment work?

All weekly sessions are booked and paid on ClassForKids as a monthly subscription, and most start with a free trial. 1-to-1 coaching can be paid monthly on ClassForKids or as a block of 5 or 10 sessions. JPL and grassroots teams start with a trial — request one on the team pages.

Do you run girls-only sessions?

Yes — girls-only group sessions for ages 7–13 run on Thursdays, 6–7pm at Tuffley Rovers Football Club.

Who are elite groups for?

Ambitious, committed players ready for high-tempo, challenging sessions. Groups run on Fridays at Tuffley Rovers FC for U7–U9, U10–U12 and U13–U16.

What is JPL?

The Junior Premier League — a competitive league for players ready to take the step up from grassroots. We run JPL teams from U9 to U12. Find out more.

Do you offer goalkeeper coaching?

Yes — goalkeeper groups for ages 6–18 run on Fridays 7–8pm at Tuffley Rovers FC and Sundays 3–4pm at Beaufort Playing Fields, plus specialist 1-to-1 goalkeeper coaching.

Are your coaches qualified and checked?

All our coaches are enhanced DBS checked and safeguarding trained, sessions are fully insured with public liability cover and first aid compliant, and we are FA accredited.

    Privacy notice

    KC Coaching Development Ltd

    We collect the details you give us when you book or enquire: your name, phone number and email, and your child's name, date of birth, club, experience and any medical information or additional needs.

    Why we use it

    • To manage bookings and contact you about sessions
    • To place your child in the right age group
    • To keep your child safe — medical details are shared only with the coaches running their session
    • Photos and videos are only used on social media if you've given consent

    How it's kept

    Booking details are sent securely to our team by email or WhatsApp, and payments are handled by ClassForKids. We don't sell your data or share it for marketing. We keep it only as long as your child trains with us, plus any period required for insurance or safeguarding records.

    Your rights

    You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing kccoaching@outlook.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
